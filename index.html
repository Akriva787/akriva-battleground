FIELD MANUAL

Cover is your teammate.

Mobile: Use the left joystick to move. Swipe the scene with another finger to aim. Hold FIRE to shoot; drag FIRE to aim while shooting. Tap Prone to lie down and crawl; tap it again to stand. Tap Jump to leap. Aim toggles zoom. Open Support for loot, missiles, medkits and ambulance actions. Settings opens graphics and audio controls. Landscape is recommended. All views start in TPP.

Move: Use WASD, arrow keys or the on-screen movement buttons. Press Space or tap Jump to jump. You can move and shoot in the air. Jumping is unavailable while driving; land before jumping again.

Aim: Move the mouse to aim with Mouse aim ON. Left-click to fire; hold right-click to aim. Clicking the scene captures the mouse in supported browsers for continuous turning. Press Esc to release the cursor and pause. If capture is unavailable, hold and drag the scene to aim, or retry with Capture mouse. On touch screens, drag to aim.

Mouse-only movement: Enable Auto run and steer with the mouse. Disable it to stop. You can also use the on-screen direction buttons. Hold Shift to sprint.

Graphics: Choose Low if the game feels slow. All modes target 60 FPS. Adaptive resolution reduces rendering resolution when performance drops; Low also limits the pixel count on larger displays.

Fire: Left-click or hold FIRE to shoot. Use Aim or hold right-click to aim. Place the crosshair over an enemy for accurate shots.

Reload: Press R to reload. Magazine capacity and reload duration depend on your weapon. Press E near an ammo crate to collect ammunition. Green medical crates restore health.

Operation Blackout: Select the infiltration mode before deployment. Recover the warehouse keycard, disable guard communications, download intelligence, then return south to extraction. Hold F or INTERACT at each marked station while standing still and not taking damage. The gold minimap marker shows your current objective. Kills alone do not finish the mission; there is no shrinking zone in this mode.

Prone: Press Z or tap Prone to lie down. Move slowly, aim and fire while prone. Tap again to stand, C to crouch, or Jump to stand and leap. Stance changes need clear space and are unavailable while airborne or driving.

Stealth: C toggles crouch, lowers the camera and slows movement. Quiet crouched movement reduces guard detection range. Gunfire can be heard. An alarm above 60 lets the active guard radio share your last known position; disabling the communications terminal stops those broadcasts.

India Expedition: Select India Expedition in Operations Hub. Explore the full country outline, 36 state/UT hubs and islands. N or Support → India Atlas opens transit destinations. Cars, SUVs, pickups, bikes, scooters and auto rickshaws are parked at hubs. V enters/exits nearby vehicles. WASD/joystick drives, and Jump/Space becomes Brake. This free-roam mode has no shrinking zone or AI combat; existing combat modes retain their arena.

Buildings: Warehouse front doorways are open. Walk inside, collect supplies and fire through windows. Solid wall sections and roofs stop shots; windowsills stop low shots. The third-person camera moves closer when a wall is behind you.

Music: Original tactical background theme starts on deployment. Music on/off and its volume slider are separate from weapon sounds. Music pauses with the game and when hidden, and becomes quieter while firing or taking damage.

Combat feel: Sound starts enabled and can be muted with Sound off. Weapons have distinct synthesized shot sounds. First-person weapons kick and lower during reloads; impacts on cover fade after a few seconds. Crosshair spread briefly responds to recoil.

Enemies: Bots investigate nearby gunfire, remember last seen positions briefly, flank visible targets, seek cover when injured and recover health after reaching safety.

Survive: In Battle Royale, solid buildings and containers block enemy shots. The minimap circle marks the safe zone. It starts shrinking after 90 seconds; staying outside causes damage. Operation Blackout has no shrinking zone.

Equipment: Before deployment, Equipment opens the 3,000-credit budget. Switch guns with 1–4 or the Gun selector. Each gun has separate ammunition; a new gun is bought once per match with credits. Eliminations earn 150 credits.

Support: G launches a selected missile (two per loadout). H deploys your selected ambulance once in clear space; stand nearby to heal, or press V within 4m to drive/exit. M uses a purchased medkit. Armor absorbs part of incoming bot damage.

Character asset: Textured Vanguard soldier and animation data adapted from the three.js / Mixamo soldier example. Photographic ground texture: Dirt / Poly Haven (CC0). Original gameplay and map layout.

There are nine AI guards. Visible enemies have health bars. This is an offline solo prototype with original gameplay and credited third-party visual assets. Online multiplayer is not included.
