# Weapon roster

52 named firearms across AR, SMG, DMR, sniper, shotgun, LMG and pistol groups, plus the four original Akriva weapons for compatibility. Every gun is free; M416 is the default. Open Settings → Loadout to equip; Equipment chooses the starting gun within the 3,000-credit budget. The preview selector uses the same catalogue without purchasing.

Ammo, fire rate, magazine, reload, recoil and credit costs are original Akriva gameplay tuning. Auto guns fire while held; single-shot guns require each press. Server and browser share weapons.js. Online weapons are selectable under the existing server rules; there is no online credit shop. Offline purchased guns retain individual ammo.

Gun meshes and synthetic sounds are original family-based approximations. They are not PUBG models, sounds or exact stats. Several guns share base geometry. No claim of complete PUBG-version parity: Flare Gun, throwables and melee weapons are not part of this firearm update. “Kar 20” is read as the Mk20-H DMR, added alongside ACE32, Honey Badger, Dragunov, Lynx AMR and JS9; Kar98K and M24 are also included.
